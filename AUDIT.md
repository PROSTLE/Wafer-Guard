## Recommendation: **NO-GO**

The supplied notebook reports **F1 = 0.9915**, but that number is measured on a test set that is
50 % defects, contains SMOTE copies of training rows, and mixes past and future wafers.
Measured honestly (trained on the past, tested on the most recent 313 wafers at their natural
defect rate of 5.4 %), the repaired model reaches **F1 = 0.129**
(95 % bootstrap CI 0.000–0.294; 5-seed mean 0.125 ± 0.028)
and **PR-AUC = 0.095** against a no-skill baseline of 0.054.
The release gate (F1 ≥ 0.9) is **not met**.

## Q1 · The three silent bugs, and how they were fixed

### 1. Data leakage (cell 8, severity: critical)
**Mechanism.** Imputer and scaler were fit on all 1567 wafers; SMOTE oversampled *before*
the split; the split was random on a time series.

**Evidence (from our runs).**
- After SMOTE the test set is 50 % defects (natural rate 6.64 %);
  46.8 % of test rows and 93.5 % of test defects are synthetic.
- Median distance from a test row to its nearest training row: real passes 17.77,
  real defects 0.99. For 100 %
  of real test defects that nearest neighbour is a synthetic SMOTE row, and for
  100 % it lies exactly on a SMOTE interpolation line starting at
  that test defect (max gap 4.4e-15), i.e. it was generated *from* the test wafer.
- 100 % of real test wafers have training wafers from their future
  (median 686 per test wafer).
- Ablation: re-introducing only SMOTE-before-split into the repaired pipeline gives F1
  0.993; only the random split gives
  0.272 (repaired: 0.125).

**Fix.** Chronological split: train 2008-07-19 → 2008-09-20
(940 wafers), validation → 2008-10-02 (314),
test → 2008-10-17 (313); equal timestamps never straddle a boundary. All statistics are fit on training wafers only (`WaferPreprocessor`).
SMOTE is applied to the training set only. Test stays at natural prevalence.

### 2. Corrupted loss gradients (cells 10 / 12 / 14, severity: high)
**Mechanism.** Inputs are `[64, 1, 590]`, reconstructions `[64, 590]`;
`(reconstructed - batch_x)` silently broadcasts to `[64, 64, 590]`, comparing every reconstruction with
every input in the batch.

**Evidence.** On one real batch the buggy loss scores a *flawless* reconstruction at
1.158 but a constant "average wafer" at
0.579, so it rewards the wrong answer. The original model's reconstructions vary
0.005× as much as its inputs (collapsed to a constant). In the ablation,
reconstruction spread is 0.004 with the bug vs 0.503 repaired.

**Fix.** `reconstruction_loss` flattens the target to `[B, F]` and `assert`s equal shapes; the classifier loss asserts
`logits.shape == labels.shape`. A shape mismatch now stops training instead of corrupting it.

### 3. Invariant blindness to the legacy Probe Station PS-L (severity: high)
**Mechanism.** 146 wafers (9.3 %) carry `sensor_0 == 0.0` and firmware codes in
`sensor_1` (100: 61, 200: 35, 300: 50); the codes appear on no other wafer.
The original averaged and scaled them as real measurements.

**Evidence.** The imputer's fill value for `sensor_1` became 2280.5
(real readings average 2495.7). The scaler's σ became
878.8 for `sensor_0` and 674.2 for
`sensor_1`, versus 74.3 and 79.8 for real
readings, so genuine readings were squashed to a spread of 0.085σ and
0.118σ. The three codes collapsed to 100 → -3.23σ, 200 → -3.09σ, 300 → -2.94σ.

**Fix.** `ps_l_legacy` flag plus one-hot `ps_l_fw_code_100/200/300`; the sentinel and codes are removed from the
continuous columns before any statistic is computed and never scaled; their continuous slots are neutral (0) and
flagged, not filled with a pretend measurement. On this dataset the score change is within seed noise
(F1 0.100 ± 0.092 blind vs
0.125 ± 0.028 repaired); it is fixed because the input was wrong by construction.

### Other defects found
- **Fixed 0.5 threshold.** The model is over-confident (final training classification loss 0.00025);
  at 0.5 it scores 2 test wafers as defects, 0 of them
  real (ablation F1 at 0.5: 0.000). Threshold is now tuned on validation, then frozen.
- **Drift hazards.** 122 sensors are constant in training (dropped). With
  train-only statistics, test values reach 37152σ; 612 of
  139463 test cells exceed ±5.0σ and are clipped.
- **Shipped artifacts** were the leaky imputer/scaler; replaced by `artifacts/preprocessor_repaired.joblib`.

## Residual risks
1. **Small numbers.** The test block has 17 defects and validation 11; intervals are wide and the
   threshold is noisy.
2. **PS-L shortcut.** 0 of 146 PS-L wafers failed in this data. The model can learn "PS-L → pass";
   in test it flagged 0 of 26 PS-L wafers.
   The data contains no failing PS-L wafer, so the model's ability to catch a real PS-L excursion is untested.
3. **Drift.** Defect rate and sensor distributions move month to month; periodic re-fitting and monitoring are required.
4. **Model value.** A logistic regression on the same features reaches PR-AUC 0.088 vs 0.095 for the autoencoder.

## Go / no-go
**NO-GO.** The original passed the 0.9 gate only on a leaky, balanced test (F1 0.992). After repair the model ranks wafers better than chance (PR-AUC 0.095 vs 0.054) but its F1 of 0.129 is below the gate. Before any release: collect more labelled excursions (especially from PS-L), validate on a longer future window, and restate the gate in terms the line cares about (e.g. recall at a tolerable false-alarm rate).
